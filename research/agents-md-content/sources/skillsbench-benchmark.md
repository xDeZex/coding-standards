# SkillsBench: Benchmarking How Well Agent Skills Work Across Diverse Tasks

Preprint, arXiv 2602.12670, submitted 13 Feb 2026 (v4 14 Jun 2026). Abstract page plus search-result snippets, via summarising tools.

- 87 tasks in 8 domains, 18 model-harness configurations, paired with and without curated skills, deterministic verifiers.
- Curated skills raised average pass rate from 33.9% to 50.5% (+16.6 points).
- Focused bundles of two to three skills beat both minimal and exhaustive collections.
- Search snippets (not verified on the paper itself) add that some human-authored skills are rarely invoked and their absence does not affect success, and that poorly designed YAML descriptions make skills hard to trigger.
- Relevance: evidence that skills can help on knowledge or procedure the model lacks, when curated. Not about test-running, and not a comparison against AGENTS.md.
