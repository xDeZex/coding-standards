# Readers Prefer Outputs of AI Trained on Copyrighted Books over Expert Human Writers

- Authors: Chakrabarty, Ginsburg, Dhillon. Revised 2026-03. Three frontier models emulating 50 award-winning authors, passages up to 450 words.
- With standard prompting, expert (MFA-trained) readers strongly preferred human writing for style. After fine-tuning on an author's complete works, ChatGPT outputs were rated superior by expert readers on style and quality, and general readers preferred them even more.
- Reports detection as AI fell from 97% (prompted) to 3% (fine-tuned); average fine-tuning cost about 81 USD per author.
- Legal framing (copyright fair use) is part of the paper. Read via abstract page only; whether this is peer reviewed was not checked.
