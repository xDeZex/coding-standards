# When A Helpful Assistant Is Not Really Helpful: Personas in System Prompts Do Not Improve Performances of LLMs

- Authors: Zheng, Pei, Logeswaran, Lee, Jurgens. Findings of EMNLP 2024. 162 roles, 4 model families, 2,410 factual questions.
- Adding personas did not improve accuracy over no persona; picking the best persona per question was hard, automated selection often no better than random.
- Scope is factual question answering, not writing style or voice, so it does not test persona effects on prose.
- Read through the arXiv abstract page via a page-to-Markdown summariser; the full paper was not read, so numbers below are the abstract-level claims only.
