# Artificial Hivemind: The Open-Ended Homogeneity of Language Models

- Authors: Jiang et al. (incl. Yejin Choi). NeurIPS 2025 Datasets and Benchmarks, oral.
- Introduces Infinity-Chat: 26,000 open-ended queries with a taxonomy of 6 categories and 17 subcategories, plus over 31,000 human annotations.
- Finds pronounced mode collapse within a model and, more strongly, similarity across different models ("Artificial Hivemind").
- Finds that language models, reward models and LLM judges align poorly with human judgments when responses draw varied individual preferences, even though overall quality is acceptable.
