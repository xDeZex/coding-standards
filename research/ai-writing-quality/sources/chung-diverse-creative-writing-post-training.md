# Modifying Large Language Model Post-Training for Diverse Creative Writing

- Authors: Chung, Padmakumar, Roemmele, Sun, Kreminski.
- Adds a "deviation" term (how different a sample is from others for the same prompt) to DPO and ORPO training.
- Reports an 8B model with diversity comparable to human-created datasets and output quality on par with GPT-4o and DeepSeek-R1, checked by human evaluation and ablations, and compared with DivPO.
