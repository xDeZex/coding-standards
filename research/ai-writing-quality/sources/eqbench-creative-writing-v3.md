# EQ-Bench Creative Writing v3 (leaderboard and About page)

- Source pages: https://eqbench.com/creative_writing.html and https://eqbench.com/about.html (first-party; both undated).
- Two scores: a rubric score (Claude Sonnet 4 via OpenRouter as judge) and an Elo from pairwise matchups judged by Claude Sonnet 4.6. Judge criteria include avoiding cliches, flowery verbosity and gratuitous metaphor.
- Also reports a repetition metric (frequency of top words, bigrams and trigrams) and a slop score matched against a list of over-represented LLM words and phrases, derived from generated stories and external slop lists.
- Stated controlled biases: length (outputs truncated to 4000 characters), position (both orders averaged). Stated uncontrolled biases: self-bias toward Anthropic models, positivity, stylistic preferences, bias towards some slop phrases.
- The About page gave no validation against human preference or Arena for v3 in the fetched content.
- Read through a summariser; wording is paraphrase except where quoted.
