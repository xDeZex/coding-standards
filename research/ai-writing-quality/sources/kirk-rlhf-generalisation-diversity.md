# Understanding the Effects of RLHF on LLM Generalisation and Diversity

- Authors: Kirk et al. Tested SFT, reward modelling and RL stages on two base models, for summarisation and instruction following.
- RLHF generalises better than SFT out of distribution, but substantially reduces output diversity relative to SFT across several diversity measures.
