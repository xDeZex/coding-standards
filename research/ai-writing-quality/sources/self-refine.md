# Self-Refine: Iterative Refinement with Self-Feedback

- Authors: Madaan et al. One model generates, critiques and revises its own output; no training.
- Seven tasks (dialogue generation to maths reasoning) with GPT-3.5, ChatGPT and GPT-4. Outputs preferred by humans and automatic metrics; about 20% absolute average improvement in task performance.
- The abstract page names no limitations and does not single out prose quality; the task set is mixed, so the figure is not a writing-quality figure.
