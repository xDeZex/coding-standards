# Judging LLM-as-a-Judge with MT-Bench and Chatbot Arena

- Authors: Zheng et al. Reports GPT-4 judges agree with human preferences over 80%, the same level as human-human agreement.
- Names position bias, verbosity bias, self-enhancement bias and limited reasoning as failure modes.
- Tasks are general chat, not creative writing specifically.
