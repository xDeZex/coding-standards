# Anthropic: Prompting Claude Fable 5.1 (writing density section)

- Vendor documentation, read from the saved full page text.
- Says the model's writing has few stock phrases and little unexplained jargon but can be denser than its predecessor (longer sentences, fewer paragraph breaks).
- Recommends defining the anti-pattern in the prompt: a paragraph describing "mannered prose" (metaphor and flourish substituting for direct statement, with paired examples) placed in the user message, or the short form "Please remove all mannered prose."
- Notes earlier anti-formatting rules should be removed or replaced because this model under-formats.
- For a quoting failure, recommends one complete worked example with a rationale sentence.
- No measurements are given; this is the vendor's own recommendation for its own model.
