# Verbalized Sampling: How to Mitigate Mode Collapse and Unlock LLM Diversity

- Authors: Zhang, Yu, Chong, Sicilia, Tomz, Manning, Shi. Submitted 2025-10-01, revised to v4 in 2026-07.
- Claims typicality bias in preference data is a fundamental cause of reduced diversity in post-trained (aligned) models.
- Proposes Verbalized Sampling (VS), a training-free prompt: ask the model to verbalise a distribution, for example "Generate 5 jokes about coffee and their corresponding probabilities".
- Reports 1.6-2.1x diversity gains over direct prompting on poems, stories and jokes, without loss of factual accuracy or safety; larger models gain more.
