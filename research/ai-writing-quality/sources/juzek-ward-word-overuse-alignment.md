# Word Overuse and Alignment in Large Language Models

- Authors: Juzek and Ward. BIAS 2025 workshop.
- Traces overuse of words like "delve" to learning from human feedback (RLHF and DPO). In their replication of alignment, participants systematically preferred text variants containing those words.
- Says raters' training-time preferences may diverge from what actual users prefer.
