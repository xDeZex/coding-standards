# WritingBench: A Comprehensive Benchmark for Generative Writing

- Authors: Wu et al. (Alibaba-affiliated authors). Six core domains, 100 subdomains.
- Query-dependent evaluation: an LLM generates instance-specific criteria, and a fine-tuned critic model scores style, format and length.
- Reports a 7B model trained on data filtered with the framework surpassing GPT-4o on the benchmark. Agreement of the critic with humans was not captured in the abstract-level read.
