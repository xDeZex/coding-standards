# Large Language Models Cannot Self-Correct Reasoning Yet

- Authors: Huang et al. ICLR 2024.
- Finds LLMs struggle to self-correct without external feedback, and performance sometimes degrades after self-correction. Scope is reasoning tasks, not prose.
