# Length-Controlled AlpacaEval

- Authors: Dubois, Galambosi, Liang, Hashimoto. COLM 2024.
- Automatic evaluators favour longer outputs. A regression-based length control raised correlation with Chatbot Arena from 0.94 to 0.98 and made the metric harder to game by lengthening.
