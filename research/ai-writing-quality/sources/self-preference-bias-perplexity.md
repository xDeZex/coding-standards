# Self-Preference Bias in LLM-as-a-Judge

- Authors: Wataoka, Takahashi, Ri. NeurIPS 2024 workshop paper.
- LLM judges give significantly higher scores to lower-perplexity outputs than humans do; the bias tracks text familiarity, not authorship by the judge itself.
- A web-search summary (not fetched at source) reported a further creative-writing study in which LLM judges gave their own stories no variance and human stories lower, more variable scores; that claim is not verified at its source.
