# Can AI writing be salvaged? Mitigating Idiosyncrasies and Improving Human-AI Alignment through Edits

- Authors: Chakrabarty, Laban, Wu. CHI 2025.
- Professional writers annotated LLM text and produced a seven-category taxonomy of recurring problems (for example cliches, unnecessary exposition). LAMP corpus: over 1,000 AI paragraphs edited by writers.
- Reports no LLM tested (GPT-4o, Claude-3.5-Sonnet, Llama-3.1-70b) outperformed the others in writing quality.
- Experts generally preferred human edits; automatic editing methods showed promise for narrowing the gap. Effect sizes not captured in the abstract-level read.
