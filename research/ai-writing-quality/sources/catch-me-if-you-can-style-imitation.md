# Catch Me If You Can? Not Yet: LLMs Still Struggle to Imitate the Implicit Writing Styles of Everyday Authors

- Authors: Wang, Tripto, Park, Li, Zhou. EMNLP 2025 Findings. Over 40,000 generations, 400+ real authors, in-context learning from the author's samples, judged by authorship attribution/verification, style matching and AI detection.
- Imitation works for formal writing (news, email) and fails for informal writing (blogs, forums). The number of demonstration examples matters.
- A search-result summary (not the paper) gave blog authorship-verification accuracy as low as 17-21%; that figure is unverified at the paper.
