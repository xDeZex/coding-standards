# Does Writing with Language Models Reduce Content Diversity?

- Authors: Padmakumar and He. ICLR 2024. Controlled experiment: argumentative essays written with GPT-3, InstructGPT or no model.
- InstructGPT assistance significantly reduced diversity and made different authors' essays more similar; GPT-3 did not.
- The loss came mainly from the model's text being less varied, not from changes in users' own contributions.
- Authors conclude that quality gains from human-feedback tuning may cost homogeneity.
