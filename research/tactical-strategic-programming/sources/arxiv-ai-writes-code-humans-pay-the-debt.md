# AI Writes Code, Humans Pay the Debt (registered report)

Preprint by Coppola, Esposito, Kazman and Lenarduzzi. Abstract page read via a summariser.

- It is a registered report: a study plan, with no results yet. It uses the SQuaD dataset of 628k issue tickets, generating agent implementations and comparing them with developer commits on static-analysis metrics.
- It only states expectations: variability across LLMs and trade-offs in issue localization and technical-debt introduction.
- Do not cite it as evidence of an effect. Recorded because its title is often echoed and it may yield results later.
