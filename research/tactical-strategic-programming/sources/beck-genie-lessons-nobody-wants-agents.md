# Genie Lessons: Nobody Wants Agents (Kent Beck)

Primary, opinion and experience post. Fetched through a summariser.

- "Nobody wants agents. Nobody wants agent swarms." Multi-agent setups push coordination onto the human.
- Beck says he ended up "managing" which agent did what, "holding state in [his] head that the system should have been holding."
- He argues for outcome-orientation: describe the desired result and let the AI assess feasibility.
- "Five agents can work on this codebase simultaneously. Five people can't. That's backwards." He names multi-human steering as the unsolved problem.
- Bearing on the split: the human role is coordination and outcome-setting (strategic, roles-and-process level), which fits the split, but Beck presents it as a burden and an unsolved problem rather than a settled division of labour.
