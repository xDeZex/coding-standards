# 2025 DORA State of AI-assisted Software Development report

Vendor-run survey research (Google Cloud / DORA). Read through the launch blog and the dora.dev landing page, both summarised by a tool; the full report was not read, so numbers and the capability list are unchecked. The blog summary listed only six of seven capabilities.

- Survey of nearly 5,000 technology professionals plus over 100 hours of qualitative data (per search-result summaries). Self-reported.
- AI adoption is positively associated with software delivery throughput and product performance, unlike the prior year.
- AI adoption still has a negative relationship with delivery stability: "Without robust control systems, like strong automated testing, mature version control practices, and fast feedback loops, an increase in change volume leads to instability."
- Loosely coupled architectures and fast feedback loops see gains; tightly coupled systems and slow processes see little.
- Headline: "AI's primary role is as an amplifier, magnifying an organization's existing strengths and weaknesses"; "the greatest returns on AI investment come not from the tools themselves, but from a strategic focus on the underlying organizational system."
- Capabilities model includes: clear AI policies, connecting AI to internal context, foundational practices, safety nets, internal platform, focus on end users.
- Bearing on the split: supports the strategic side (architecture, platform, process determine outcomes). It is survey-based association, not a test of who does tactical work.
