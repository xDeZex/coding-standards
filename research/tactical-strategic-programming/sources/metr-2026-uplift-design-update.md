# We are Changing our Developer Productivity Experiment Design (METR)

Primary: METR's follow-up to its 2025 RCT. Fetched through a summariser.

- New experiment from August 2025: 57 developers, 143 repositories, 800+ tasks; 10 returning and 47 new. Pay dropped to $50/hour from $150.
- Estimates: returning developers -18% (CI -38% to +9%, negative meaning a speedup as METR reports it in the summary), new developers -4% (CI -15% to +9%). The sign convention was not checked against the post; read the post before quoting numbers.
- Selection problem: "30% to 50% of developers told us that they were choosing not to submit some tasks because they did not want to do them without AI."
- METR says the data gives "only very weak evidence" and is "likely a bad proxy for the real productivity impact". It believes AI productivity is improving but can no longer measure it with this design, and plans new designs.
- Bearing on the split: no reliable measurement that AI speeds up tactical work in 2026; developers' own refusal to work without AI is behavioural evidence they find it valuable, but it is not a measurement.
