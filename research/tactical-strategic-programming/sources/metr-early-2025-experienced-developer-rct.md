# Measuring the Impact of Early-2025 AI on Experienced Open-Source Developer Productivity (METR)

Primary: randomized controlled trial by METR. Fetched through a summariser.

- Design: 16 experienced developers on large open-source repositories they knew; 246 real issues (about 2 hours each) randomly assigned to allow or disallow AI. Tools mainly Cursor Pro with Claude 3.5/3.7 Sonnet.
- Result: with AI, developers took 19% longer to complete issues.
- Perception gap: developers expected a 24% speedup and, after the slowdown, still believed AI had sped them up by 20%.
- Code quality was reported as comparable across conditions.
- Caveats from METR: the results are for experienced developers on familiar codebases, not general; tooling and prompting may not have been optimal.
- Bearing on the split: contradicts a strong form of "AI is good at tactical work" for this setting, since tactical tasks (issues in familiar repos) were slower, and shows self-reports are unreliable. Small sample, early tools; superseded in part by the 2026 follow-up (see the metr-2026-uplift-design-update note).
