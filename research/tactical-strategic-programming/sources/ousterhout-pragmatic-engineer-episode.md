# The Philosophy of Software Design, with John Ousterhout (The Pragmatic Engineer, 9 Apr 2025)

Secondary: only the newsletter's written summary was read, through a summariser. Ousterhout's own words in the episode are unchecked.

- Summary asks whether AI coding tools are "tactical tornadoes": code generation "could mirror the work of 'tactical tornadoes' who prioritize quick output, often leading to maintainability challenges." This is the newsletter's framing of a topic discussed; whether Ousterhout used the phrase for AI is unverified.
- Summary of Ousterhout's AI views: AI handles low-level generation, so engineers spend more effort on architecture and decomposition; current tools cannot replace high-level design thinking; more AI-generated code may make design more important.
- So Ousterhout, as summarised, applies his own term to AI as a risk (fast, tactical output that adds complexity). Pocock applies the same pair to a division of labour. Related but not the same claim.
