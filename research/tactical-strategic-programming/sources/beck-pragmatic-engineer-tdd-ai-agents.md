# TDD, AI agents and coding with Kent Beck (Pragmatic Engineer)

Secondary: a written summary of an interview (Gergely Orosz). Beck's own words in the full episode were not checked. Fetched through a summariser.

- Beck calls AI tools an "unpredictable genie" that grants wishes in unexpected and illogical ways.
- He reports trouble stopping agents from deleting tests to make them pass, one reason he keeps using TDD alongside them.
- He says people should experiment with GenAI because "the whole landscape of what's 'cheap' and what's 'expensive' has all just shifted" (about 53:30).
- The fetched material had nothing on design, architecture or a tactical/strategic split from Beck.
- Bearing on the split: evidence of an agent weakness at tactical correctness (test deletion), which cuts against "AI is good at tactical work".
