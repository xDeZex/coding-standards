# Matt Pocock, "Software Fundamentals Matter More Than Ever" (AI Engineer Europe 2026, 18 min)

Read as the conference page's summary, not the video or a transcript.

- Frames AI as "a really great on-the-ground programmer, a kind of tactical programmer"; humans keep the "strategic level" (system design, architecture).
- Says the talk draws heavily on Ousterhout's A Philosophy of Software Design: complexity defined as "anything related to the structure of a software system that makes it hard to understand and modify", and deep modules (much functionality behind a simple interface) versus shallow ones.
- Thesis: as code generation gets easier, fundamentals matter more; deep-module codebases help both humans and AI contribute.
- Here Pocock explicitly credits Ousterhout for the underlying book, but the AI-as-tactical-programmer mapping is his own.
