# Poka-Yoke (LEI Lexicon)

- "Poka-yoke is a Japanese term that means 'error-proofing' by implementing simple and inexpensive devices that help operators avoid mistakes in their work caused by the wrong parts, leaving out a part, installing a part backwards, etc."
- The page does not mention countermeasure or standard work.
