# What Problems Are Lean Practices? Part 1 of 2 (Michael Balle, LEI Lean Post)

Secondary. Read via WebFetch summary.

- Countermeasures respond to gaps between standard and actual conditions. Immediate countermeasures have two purposes: "(1) protect the customer and (2) return to normal conditions." Example: a quality complaint triggers the standard response of installing a 100% check at the end of the process.
- For recurring problems, an eight-step PDCA approach: 1 clarify the problem, 2 grasp the situation, 3 set a target, 4 analyze the root cause, 5 develop countermeasures, 6 see countermeasures through, 7 evaluate both results and process, 8 standardize successful processes.
- Standardizing successful processes prevents regression and spreads learning.
