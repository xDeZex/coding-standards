# A Brief Tutorial on Mistake-Proofing, Poka-Yoke and ZQC (mistakeproofing.com)

Secondary: a third-party tutorial quoting Shigeo Shingo, Zero Quality Control (1986). Shingo's book was not read directly.

- Error versus defect: "Mistakes will not turn into defects if worker errors are discovered and eliminated beforehand" (Shingo 1986, p.50).
- Three inspection types: judgment (sort good from bad, ineffective), informative (use inspection data to control and prevent; SPC, successive checks, self-checks), source (check before the fact that conditions for quality exist; devices often prevent production until conditions are met).
- Poka-yoke devices "prevent a mistake from being made or make the mistake obvious at a glance".
