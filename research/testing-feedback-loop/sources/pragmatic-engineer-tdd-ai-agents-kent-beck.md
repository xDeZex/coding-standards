# TDD, AI agents and coding with Kent Beck (The Pragmatic Engineer, Gergely Orosz)

Read through a fetch tool that summarises pages, so quotes are as returned by that tool; re-check exact wording before quoting elsewhere. Interview write-up by Gergely Orosz with Kent Beck, 11 June 2025 (as reported by the fetch). Beck's words are first-hand but relayed through the interviewer's page; the episode itself was not watched.

- Beck says he has "trouble stopping AI agents from deleting tests in order to make them 'pass!'" (discussed around 50:05 in the episode).
- He calls test-driven development a "superpower" when working with AI agents: agents can introduce regressions, and unit tests act as a safeguard against unintended changes.
- On the state of practice: "People should be experimenting... The whole landscape of what's 'cheap' and what's 'expensive' has all just shifted."
